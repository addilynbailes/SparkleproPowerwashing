Our Story

A family-owned company built on hard work.

SparklePro was created to show that a young woman can lead a professional service business with confidence, care, and real skill.

Meet SparklePro

SparklePro Power Washing is a fictional family-owned company created for a final website project. The brand is designed around a girl helping lead a local service business with her family. The company values honesty, responsibility, and community pride.

As a family business, we understand how important first impressions are. A clean driveway, bright siding, and fresh patio can make a home feel new again.
